## 7. Statistical methodology

For each experiment/algorithm/experimental-condition cell and each of delivery ratio, propagation delay, duplicates, and total forwards, S6 calculated `n`, arithmetic mean, sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`), and a Student-t 95% confidence interval. With `n=20`, the interval was `mean ± scipy.stats.t.ppf(0.975, 19) × s/sqrt(20)`. The critical value was derived programmatically; 1.96 was not substituted.

## 8. Independent mean calculation

All 42 × 4 means were calculated directly from `final_control_raw_canonical.csv`, not copied from the S5 summary. Status: PASS.

## 9. Independent sample-SD calculation

All 168 sample standard deviations were independently calculated with `ddof=1`. There are 67 valid zero-SD cell/metrics: 27 delivery-ratio, 22 duplicates, and 18 total-forwards cases. Propagation delay has no zero-SD cells. No variability was manufactured. Status: PASS.

## 10. Independent Student-t 95% CI calculation

All 168 intervals are finite and satisfy `CI low <= mean <= CI high`. Status: PASS.

## 11. Cross-check against S5

S6 joined its 42 independently calculated rows to the frozen S5 summary by experiment, algorithm, and condition. It compared `n`, mean, standard deviation, CI low, and CI high using `rtol=1e-10`, `atol=1e-12`. All values agree. Maximum absolute difference: `2.8421709430404007e-14`. Status: PASS.

## 12. Variability validation
